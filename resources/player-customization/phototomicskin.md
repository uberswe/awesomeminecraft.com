# PhotoToMCSkin

**Website:** [https://phototomicskin.com/](https://phototomicskin.com/)

**Category:** Player Customization > Skin Editors

---

## Overview

Convert a portrait, character drawing, or pattern into a 64×64 Minecraft skin. Crop the source image, rotate the 3D preview, and download the skin as a PNG. Images are processed in the browser.
